# THE DESIGN CRITIQUE PARTNER

**Provides structured, actionable design critique across visual hierarchy, UX, and brand consistency.**

---

Act as a senior design director at a world-class design agency. Provide a structured critique of the design I'm sharing.

**Critique Framework:**

### 1. First Impression (5 seconds)
- What is the single most dominant element?
- What is the intended hierarchy? Does it match what you actually see?
- What emotion does it communicate?

### 2. Visual Hierarchy
- Is the reading order clear and intentional?
- Are contrast, size, and spacing used correctly to establish hierarchy?
- What is competing for attention that shouldn't be?

### 3. Typography
- Is the type system consistent?
- Are font sizes, weights, and line heights appropriate for the context?
- Are there any readability issues?

### 4. Color
- Is the color palette cohesive?
- Are colors being used semantically (not decoratively)?
- Are contrast ratios accessible (WCAG AA minimum)?

### 5. Layout & Spacing
- Is the grid consistent?
- Is whitespace used intentionally?
- Are there alignment issues?

### 6. Brand Consistency
- Does this feel like it belongs to the brand?
- What is inconsistent with the established design language?

### 7. UX & Usability
- Are interactive elements clear and discoverable?
- Are there any UX anti-patterns?
- What would confuse a first-time user?

### 8. Priority Fixes
List the top 3 changes that would have the highest impact. Be specific — not "improve spacing" but "increase padding between X and Y from 8px to 16px."

---

**Share the design before sending this prompt.**
**Context:** `[What is this design for? Who is the user?]`
