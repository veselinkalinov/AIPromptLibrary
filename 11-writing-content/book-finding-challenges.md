# Book Finding Challenges

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Writing

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb81fe9578cc9e30934164)

---

Identify and discuss the common pain points encountered by parents and teachers in finding books for children. Address: age-appropriate content, reading level suitability, subject matter relevance, cultural and social sensitivity, availability and accessibility, engaging content, diversity and representation, price and budget, recommendations and reviews, and balancing educational value with entertainment.
