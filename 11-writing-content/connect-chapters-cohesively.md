# Connect Chapters Cohesively

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Writing

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb81a6ad00da78e625fbcf)

---

Create a list of non-fiction writing prompts that focus on connecting different chapters cohesively in a self-help book using C. G. Jung's process. Include prompts for: Chapter Themes and Connections, Narrative and Flow with transitional sections, Integration of Exercises throughout chapters, Jungian Process Emphasis as a consistent thread, and Cohesion Strategies such as reflective journaling and mind maps.
