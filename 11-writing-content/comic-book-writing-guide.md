Create a detailed and age-appropriate guide for writing a comic book about perseverance. Include: Definition of Perseverance as a central theme, Comic Book Structure & Elements (Plot, Character, Theme, Setting, Point of View, Conflict, Hero & Antagonist, Dialogue, Pacing, Resolution), Comic Book Formatting including panel design and dialogue bubbles, and Examples & Exercises for each step.
