# Create Coloring Book Ideas

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Writing, Creative

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb817fa95aeb15e05fe2ef)

---

Create three unique ideas for a coloring book focused on labyrinths and mazes, specifically designed for teenagers. For each idea provide: the overarching theme or concept, description of types of labyrinths and mazes included, suggestions for additional elements or designs (patterns, cultural motifs, fantasy elements), and ways to incorporate educational or inspirational content that would appeal to teenagers.
