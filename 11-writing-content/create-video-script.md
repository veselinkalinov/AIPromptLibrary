# Create Video Script

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Writing, Creative

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb817db298d35725196e22)

---

Create a compelling video script for a specific topic or product. Include: engaging hook in the first 5-10 seconds, clear narrative structure with beginning/middle/end, key talking points and supporting details, calls-to-action at appropriate moments, visual cue suggestions for production, tone and pacing guidance, and platform-specific considerations for length and format (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn).
