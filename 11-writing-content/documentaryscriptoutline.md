# DocumentaryScriptOutline

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Writing

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb814197c5e3abc671baca)

---

Create a detailed documentary script outline for a compelling documentary film. Include: opening hook and narrative premise, key story arc structure with act breaks, interview subject identification and question frameworks, archival footage and visual evidence requirements, narration style and voice guidance, pacing and runtime considerations, emotional beats and audience engagement points, research requirements and fact-checking process, and distribution and audience targeting strategy.
