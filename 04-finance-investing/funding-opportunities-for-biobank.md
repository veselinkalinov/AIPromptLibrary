# Funding Opportunities for Biobank

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Analysis

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb8180ad73fd53abdbd15b)

---

Create a comprehensive guide for identifying and securing funding opportunities for a biobank research facility. Include: federal grant sources (NIH, NSF, DOD), private foundation funding opportunities, industry partnership and collaboration models, international funding sources, grant writing strategies for biobank-specific projects, budget justification templates, reporting requirements and compliance considerations, diversification of funding sources strategy, and networking with funding program officers.
