# Elevator Pitch Funding

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Marketing

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb812ca3c6ecb21b6d3a78)

---

Create a compelling elevator pitch for securing funding from investors. Include: attention-grabbing opening that states the problem being solved, clear value proposition and unique differentiator, market size and opportunity description, business model and revenue streams explanation, traction and key metrics achieved, team credentials and relevant experience, funding ask and use of funds breakdown, and call-to-action for the next steps in the investment conversation.
