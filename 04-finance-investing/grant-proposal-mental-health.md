# Grant Proposal Mental Health

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Writing

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb81ecb21ffde74a4f1df8)

---

Create a comprehensive grant proposal for a mental health program or initiative. Include: needs assessment with mental health statistics and community data, program description with evidence-based interventions, target population description and selection criteria, program goals, objectives, and measurable outcomes, staffing plan and qualifications, evaluation methodology and data collection, budget narrative with justification, organizational qualifications and capacity, sustainability plan, and letters of support from community partners.
