# Create Investment Course

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Studying

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb813c9297eebbf8b0b37c)

Create a detailed course on Investments including: Course Structure from basic to advanced, Introduction to Investment Concepts (risk vs return, diversification), Exploration of Investment Options (Treasury Bonds, Treasury Bills, Unit Trusts, Life Insurance), Investment Strategies for each vehicle, Market Analysis teaching economic indicators, Risk Management strategies, Advanced Topics (portfolio construction, tax implications, economic cycles), and Practical Applications with case studies.
