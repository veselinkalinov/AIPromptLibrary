# AI COURSE OVERVIEW

**Builds a detailed AI course for SME owners and managers with no technical background.**

---

Create a high-level course for owners and managers of small and medium enterprises (SMEs) on the topic of AI.

**Target audience:** Business owners and managers with little to no technical background in AI.

**Course requirements:**

### Structure
- Course title and description
- Learning objectives (what participants will be able to do after the course)
- Target duration: `[e.g. 4 hours / 1 day / 4 weeks]`
- Delivery format: `[In-person / Online / Hybrid]`

### Module Breakdown
For each module, provide:
- Module title and duration
- Key topics covered
- Learning outcomes
- Activities or exercises

### Core Topics to Cover (minimum)
- What AI actually is — and what it isn't (debunking hype)
- How AI is already being used in businesses like yours
- AI tools available today without technical knowledge (ChatGPT, Copilot, etc.)
- How to evaluate an AI tool or vendor
- Data privacy and AI risk basics
- Building an AI-ready team and culture
- How to start: first 3 AI use cases for your business
- ROI and how to measure AI impact

### Materials Needed
- Slide deck outline (titles and key points per slide)
- Recommended exercises and case studies
- Takeaway resources list
- Assessment or quiz if applicable

### Delivery Notes
- How to make this accessible for non-technical audiences
- Which topics tend to generate the most questions
- Common misconceptions to address proactively
