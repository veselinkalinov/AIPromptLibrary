# GenerationalDifferencesMasterclass

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Studying

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb81acaad4e18c4b765e05)

---

Create a comprehensive masterclass on generational differences in the workplace. Include: overview of generational cohorts (Silent, Boomer, Gen X, Millennial, Gen Z), characteristic values, work styles, and communication preferences of each generation, management strategies tailored to different generational needs, cross-generational team collaboration techniques, generational conflict resolution approaches, recruitment and retention strategies for each generation, and creating an inclusive multigenerational workplace culture.
