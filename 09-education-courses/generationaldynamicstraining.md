# GenerationalDynamicsTraining

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Studying

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb81e0b1b5c5827c87c37e)

---

Create a training program on generational dynamics for organizational leadership. Include: understanding generational theory and its limitations, identifying generational patterns in your organization, leadership styles that transcend generational differences, mentoring and reverse mentoring program design, technology adoption strategies across generations, communication channel preferences and strategies, performance management adaptation for different generations, succession planning across generational transitions, and measuring generational diversity and inclusion progress.
