Create a comprehensive early childhood curriculum for ages 3-6. Include: developmentally appropriate learning objectives by age group, literacy and language development activities, numeracy and mathematical thinking exercises, social-emotional learning components, creative arts and movement integration, science exploration and nature activities, daily schedule structure and transition strategies, assessment methods for young learners, parent engagement and communication strategies, and inclusive practices for diverse learners.
