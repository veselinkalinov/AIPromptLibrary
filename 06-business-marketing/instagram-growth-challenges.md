# Instagram Growth Challenges

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Marketing

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb81f8826aec866880df51)

---

Create a series of Instagram growth challenges designed to increase follower count and engagement. Include: 30-day follower growth challenge with daily tasks, engagement challenge with specific interaction goals, content creation challenge with different post types each day, hashtag research and optimization challenge, collaboration and partnership challenge for cross-promotion, Reels and video content creation challenge, Stories engagement challenge using interactive features, and metrics tracking framework to measure challenge results.
