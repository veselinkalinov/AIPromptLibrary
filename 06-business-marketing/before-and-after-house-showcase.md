# Before And After House Showcase

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Creative

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb817da8ebc07267869049)

---

Create an image prompt that can generate a realistic before-and-after showcase of a house that has undergone transformations for house raising projects on social media. Specify: initial single-story house scene with architecture details, the after transformation to two-story with new windows and modern siding, composition for clarity and continuity with identical viewpoint and lighting, and platform recommendations for Instagram and Facebook.
