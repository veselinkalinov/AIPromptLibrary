# Government Contract Opportunities

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Analysis

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb81edba83e631f982695b)

---

Create a comprehensive guide to identifying and winning government contract opportunities. Include: understanding the government procurement process, registering in SAM.gov and other required databases, identifying contract opportunities through USASpending.gov and FedBizOpps, small business set-aside programs and certifications (8a, SDVOSB, WOSB, HUBZone), proposal writing best practices for government RFPs, subcontracting opportunities with prime contractors, past performance documentation, teaming arrangements, and compliance and reporting requirements.
