# Enhance Real Estate Online Presence

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Marketing

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb815f87a1ed0bb9c1d2d7)

---

Create a comprehensive strategy to enhance a real estate agent's or agency's online presence. Include: website optimization for lead generation, local SEO strategy for real estate searches, Google Business Profile optimization, social media platform strategy (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube), content marketing plan including market reports and neighborhood guides, email marketing campaigns for leads and past clients, online review management strategy, paid advertising recommendations, and analytics and performance tracking setup.
