**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Marketing

Create a comprehensive guide on designing effective sales funnels for various business types. Include: awareness stage strategies and top-of-funnel content, lead generation and capture techniques, nurturing sequences and middle-of-funnel tactics, conversion optimization and bottom-of-funnel strategies, post-purchase retention and upsell opportunities, funnel analytics and optimization metrics, and platform-specific funnel recommendations (ClickFunnels, Kajabi, etc.).
