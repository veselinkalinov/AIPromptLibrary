# Go To Market Funnel Strategies

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Marketing

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb8175bbd3e7266160ac05)

---

Create comprehensive go-to-market funnel strategies for launching a new product or service. Include: market positioning and target audience definition, awareness building tactics and channel selection, lead generation and capture mechanisms, nurturing sequences and content strategy, sales enablement materials and collateral, conversion optimization strategies, customer success and onboarding process, referral and advocacy programs, retention and expansion strategies, and metrics and KPIs for each funnel stage.
