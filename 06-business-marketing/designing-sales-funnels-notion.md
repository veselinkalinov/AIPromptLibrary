# Designing Sales Funnels

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Marketing

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb81ac88a5d670e40acc0f)

---

Develop a comprehensive approach to designing sales funnels that convert prospects into customers. Include understanding different funnel types (webinar, product launch, lead magnet, tripwire), mapping the customer journey from awareness to purchase, copywriting strategies for each funnel stage, email sequence design for automated follow-up, landing page optimization best practices, upsell and cross-sell integration points, funnel analytics and conversion rate benchmarks, A/B testing methodology for funnel optimization, and tools and platforms for building and managing sales funnels.
