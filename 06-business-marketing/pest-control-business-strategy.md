# Pest Control Business Strategy

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Marketing

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb81388722c145cffe795f)

---

Create a comprehensive business strategy for a pest control company. Include: market analysis and target customer segmentation, service menu development and pricing strategy, local SEO and digital marketing approach, customer acquisition and retention strategies, residential vs commercial service differentiation, seasonal service promotion planning, customer education content strategy, review and reputation management, employee training and certification requirements, equipment and chemical inventory management, and expansion strategy for adding service areas or franchising.
