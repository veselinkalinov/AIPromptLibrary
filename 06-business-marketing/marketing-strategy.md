# MARKETING STRATEGY

**Builds a comprehensive marketing strategy for any product, platform, or brand.**

---

Create a comprehensive marketing strategy plan for `[PRODUCT/COMPANY]`.

**Context:**

```
Product/Company: []
Target audience: []
Current stage: [Pre-launch / Early-stage / Growth / Scale]
Budget range: []
Primary channel focus (if any): []
Key competitors: []
Unique differentiator: []
```

**The strategy must cover:**

### 1. Market Analysis
- Target audience segmentation (demographics, psychographics, behaviors)
- Buyer persona(s) — minimum 2
- Competitive landscape: positioning map and key differentiators

### 2. Positioning
- Positioning statement
- Key messages by audience segment
- Value proposition hierarchy

### 3. Channel Strategy
For each relevant channel, specify:
- Why this channel for this audience
- Content types and formats
- Posting frequency
- KPIs to track
- Estimated budget allocation %

Channels to evaluate: SEO, paid search, paid social, organic social, email, content marketing, influencer, community, events, partnerships.

### 4. Content Plan
- Content pillars (3–5 themes)
- Content formats per pillar
- 30-day content calendar outline

### 5. Funnel Strategy
- Awareness → Interest → Consideration → Decision tactics
- Lead capture mechanism
- Nurture sequence outline

### 6. Metrics & Goals
- Primary KPIs for 30 / 60 / 90 days
- How to measure each
- What success looks like at 6 months

### 7. Quick Wins
- 3 things to do in the first 2 weeks that require low effort but high impact
