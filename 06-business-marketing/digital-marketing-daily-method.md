# Digital Marketing Daily Method

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Marketing

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb8156a635d2f2cf9f7200)

---

Create a structured daily method of operation (DMO) for a digital marketer. Include: morning routine for checking analytics and metrics, content creation and scheduling tasks, engagement and community management activities, lead generation and outreach sequences, ad monitoring and optimization tasks, end-of-day reporting and planning, weekly review and strategy adjustment process, and tools and apps to streamline the daily workflow.
