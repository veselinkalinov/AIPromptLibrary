# Engagement Plan for Community

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Marketing

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb81b58922eaaf86afb936)

---

Create a detailed community engagement plan for building and nurturing an online community. Include: community platform selection and setup, onboarding experience for new members, content calendar and posting cadence, engagement tactics and conversation starters, community events and virtual gatherings, member recognition and rewards programs, moderation guidelines and community rules, growth strategies for expanding membership, metrics for measuring community health and engagement, and monetization strategies when appropriate.
