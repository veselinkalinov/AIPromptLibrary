# Create Lead Magnet

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Marketing

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb81c6bcbcff0dc928cc7b)

---

Identify a specific niche for creating a lead magnet. Understand the target audience's interests and pain points. Develop an engaging headline with a clear immediate benefit. Describe the type of lead magnet (eBook, checklist, webinar, template) and why it appeals to the audience. Outline the key content or value addressing specific problems. Suggest a visually attractive design strategy. Include a clear call to action encouraging engagement.
