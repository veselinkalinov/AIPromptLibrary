# Create Ebook Marketing Strategy

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Writing, Marketing

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb81258488f6c9fb04f470)

---

Create a comprehensive marketing strategy to promote ebooks across multiple online platforms in three categories: relationships, women-focused content, and technology. For each category provide: suitable social media platforms and marketplaces, three tailored promotional content ideas per platform, platform-specific tactics (hashtags, timing, paid ads, community engagement), visual element recommendations, and influencer/email marketing suggestions.
