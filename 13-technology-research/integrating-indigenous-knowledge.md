# Integrating Indigenous Knowledge

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Studying

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb81959b38fe69e26eb447)

---

Create a comprehensive guide on integrating Indigenous knowledge systems into educational curricula. Include: foundational principles of Indigenous knowledge systems, respectful research and consultation protocols, curriculum mapping for Indigenous content integration, decolonizing teaching practices and pedagogies, land-based and place-based learning activities, Indigenous language incorporation strategies, community partnership and elder engagement models, assessment approaches that honor Indigenous ways of knowing, staff professional development requirements, and measuring outcomes of Indigenous knowledge integration.
