# CAREER TRANSITION GUIDE

**Full career transition plan from a current role to a target direction.**

---

Create a comprehensive career transition guide for the situation described below.

**Context:**

```
Current role / background: []
Target role / direction: []
Timeline: []  (e.g. transition within 6 months)
Transferable skills: []
Biggest perceived gap: []
Geographic constraints: []
Financial constraints: []  (e.g. cannot take pay cut greater than X%)
```

**The guide must cover:**

### 1. Gap Analysis
- Skills you already have that transfer directly
- Skills you need to develop
- Experience gaps and how to bridge them
- Credential gaps (certifications, degrees) — are they actually required?

### 2. Transition Roadmap
- Phase 1: Preparation (skill-building, portfolio, network)
- Phase 2: Positioning (CV, LinkedIn, narrative)
- Phase 3: Active search (applications, interviews, offers)
- Milestones and timeline for each phase

### 3. Skill Development Plan
- Specific resources to fill each skill gap (courses, projects, reading)
- Estimated time per skill
- Priority order (highest ROI skills first)

### 4. Portfolio & Evidence Strategy
- What proof of skills to build before applying
- Projects, freelance work, or contributions that demonstrate capability
- How to present past experience in the new context

### 5. Network & Positioning
- How to build a network in the target field from scratch
- LinkedIn optimization for the transition
- How to talk about the career change in interviews

### 6. Job Search Strategy
- Target companies and why
- Application volume and cadence
- Interview preparation focus areas

### 7. Financial Bridge Plan
- How long current savings cover a potential gap period
- Part-time or freelance options during transition
- When to accept an offer vs hold out
