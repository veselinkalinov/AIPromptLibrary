# AI AUTOMATION GUIDE

**Identifies and implements AI automation opportunities across business workflows.**

---

Create a comprehensive AI automation guide for the business described below.

**Context:**

```
Business type: []
Team size: []
Current tools / tech stack: []
Biggest time sinks (manual work): []
Budget for tools: []
Technical capability of team: [Non-technical / Mixed / Technical]
```

**The guide must cover:**

### 1. Automation Opportunity Audit
- Map current workflows that involve repetitive, rule-based, or high-volume tasks
- Score each by: time saved × frequency × ease of automation
- Prioritize: which to automate first for maximum ROI

### 2. Tool Recommendations
For each automation opportunity:
- Recommended tool(s)
- Cost
- Technical difficulty to implement
- Integration with existing stack
- Expected time savings per week

### 3. Implementation Roadmap
- Phase 1 (Week 1–2): Quick wins — tools that require no technical setup
- Phase 2 (Week 3–6): Medium complexity automations
- Phase 3 (Month 2–3): Custom or integrated automations

### 4. AI Tools by Function
Map the best AI tools to each business function:
- Sales and CRM
- Marketing and content
- Customer support
- Finance and bookkeeping
- HR and recruiting
- Operations and project management
- Data analysis and reporting

### 5. Financial Planning & Investment Strategies
- Estimated monthly cost of full AI stack
- Estimated time saved per month (convert to £/€/$ value)
- Break-even timeline
- Suggest financial planning for tool rollout budget

### 6. Team Adoption Plan
- How to introduce AI tools to non-technical staff
- Training requirements
- Change management approach
- How to measure adoption

### 7. Risk & Governance
- Data privacy considerations for each tool category
- Which tasks should NOT be automated (and why)
- Human review requirements for AI outputs
