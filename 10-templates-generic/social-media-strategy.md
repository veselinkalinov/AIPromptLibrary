# SOCIAL MEDIA STRATEGY TEMPLATE

**Comprehensive social media strategy covering all major platforms.**

---

Create a comprehensive social media strategy for the brand or individual described below.

**Context:**

```
Brand / Person: []
Industry / Niche: []
Primary goal: [Awareness / Leads / Community / Sales / Personal brand]
Current presence: []  (e.g. 500 Instagram followers, no TikTok)
Target audience: []
Budget for paid social: []
Team size for social: []
```

**The strategy must cover:**

### 1. Platform Selection
For each platform (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Pinterest, Facebook):
- Include or exclude with explicit reasoning based on audience and goals
- Priority tier (Primary / Secondary / Experimental)

### 2. Content Strategy
For each primary platform:
- Content pillars (3–5 themes)
- Content formats and mix (video, carousel, static, stories, etc.)
- Posting frequency
- Optimal posting times

### 3. Content Planning
- 30-day content calendar outline (topic and format per post)
- Evergreen content bank: 10 ideas that work anytime
- Seasonal and trending content integration approach

### 4. Engagement Strategy
- Community management response guidelines
- Engagement tactics (comments, DMs, collaborations)
- UGC (user-generated content) strategy if applicable

### 5. Growth Tactics
- Organic growth levers specific to each platform
- Hashtag strategy (with examples)
- Collaboration and cross-promotion approach

### 6. Paid Social (if budget available)
- Which platforms to run ads on and why
- Campaign objective recommendations by funnel stage
- Creative direction for ads

### 7. KPIs & Reporting
- Metrics to track per platform
- Reporting cadence
- What "good" looks like at 30 / 60 / 90 days
