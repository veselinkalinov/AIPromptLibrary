# FoundationalNutritionFitnessCourse

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Studying

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb8158bc7bd5821b7fadbe)

---

Create a foundational course on nutrition and fitness for beginners. Include: macronutrient and micronutrient fundamentals, caloric needs calculation and energy balance, meal planning and preparation strategies, understanding food labels and ingredient lists, hydration guidelines and recommendations, basic exercise science principles, beginner workout programming, sleep and recovery importance, habit formation and behavior change strategies, and common nutrition myths and evidence-based corrections.
