Include: macronutrient science and metabolic function, evidence-based weight management strategies, sports nutrition and performance optimization, behavior change theories and coaching techniques, meal planning and practical nutrition guidance, exercise programming across fitness goals, special populations considerations (pregnancy, elderly, athletes), supplement evidence review, client assessment and goal-setting frameworks, and program design and progression principles.
