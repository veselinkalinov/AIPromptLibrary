# NursingStudyGuide

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Studying

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb81d798f5d1967d1a5330)

---

Create a comprehensive nursing study guide for nursing students preparing for NCLEX or clinical rotations. Include: study schedule and time management for nursing school, NCLEX question types and test-taking strategies, priority setting frameworks (Maslow's, ABC, ADPIE), medication administration and pharmacology review, nursing process and clinical decision-making, body systems review with nursing considerations, critical thinking case studies, lab values interpretation guide, care plan development frameworks, and self-care strategies for nursing students.
