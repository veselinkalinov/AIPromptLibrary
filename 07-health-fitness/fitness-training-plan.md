# FITNESS TRAINING PLAN

**Generates a comprehensive, goal-specific fitness training plan.**

---

Create a comprehensive fitness training plan tailored to the specific goals and fitness level provided.

**Profile:**

```
Goal: []  (e.g. fat loss, muscle gain, endurance, general fitness)
Current fitness level: [Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced]
Available equipment: []
Days per week available: []
Session length: []
Age: []
Any health conditions or injuries: []
```

**The plan must include:**

### 1. Initial Fitness Assessment
- Self-assessment tests to establish baseline (specify tests and how to perform them)
- How to track progress throughout the plan

### 2. Program Structure
- Training split and weekly schedule
- Periodization approach (linear, undulating, or block)
- Training phases and how long each lasts

### 3. Training Sessions
For each type of session:
- Full exercise list (sets, reps, rest, tempo)
- Warm-up and cool-down protocols
- RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion) targets

### 4. Progressive Overload
- How and when to increase difficulty
- When to deload
- How to handle missed sessions

### 5. Nutrition Alignment
- Caloric and macronutrient targets for the goal
- Pre- and post-workout nutrition
- Hydration guidelines

### 6. Recovery Protocol
- Sleep recommendations
- Active recovery sessions
- Mobility work schedule

### 7. 12-Week Roadmap
- What to expect at weeks 4, 8, 12
- How to adjust if progress stalls
