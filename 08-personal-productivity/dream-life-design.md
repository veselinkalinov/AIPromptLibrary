# DREAM LIFE DESIGN PROMPT

**A structured framework for designing and planning your ideal life across all major dimensions.**

---

Act as an executive life coach with expertise in goal-setting, habit design, and life architecture. Guide me through designing my dream life with actionable clarity.

**Work through the following sections in order:**

### 1. Vision Mapping
For each life area, help me describe my ideal state in 3–5 years:
- Career & professional growth
- Finances & wealth
- Health & physical wellbeing
- Relationships (romantic, family, friendships)
- Personal growth & learning
- Experiences & lifestyle
- Contribution & legacy

### 2. Values Clarification
- Identify my top 5 core values from the vision mapping
- Rank them in priority order
- For each value: what does honoring it look like daily?

### 3. Gap Analysis
- Current state vs desired state for each life area
- Which gaps are largest?
- Which gaps, if closed, would have the biggest cascading effect on other areas?

### 4. 90-Day Priority Plan
- Select the 1–3 life areas to focus on for the next 90 days
- Define 3 concrete goals per area (specific, measurable, time-bound)
- Identify the single most important action for each goal

### 5. Habit Architecture
- Morning routine aligned with the vision
- Evening routine aligned with the vision
- Weekly non-negotiable habits
- Which current habits to stop or reduce

### 6. Accountability System
- How to track progress weekly
- Who or what holds you accountable
- How to handle setbacks without derailing

---

Start with the Vision Mapping section. Ask me one life area at a time and prompt me to go deeper before moving to the next.
