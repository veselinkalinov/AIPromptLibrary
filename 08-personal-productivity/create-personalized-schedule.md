# Create Personalized Schedule

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** General

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb81489f80fbd3ae83ceee)

---

Act as a productivity coach and goal planner. Create a detailed and personalized daily schedule for an entire month to help stay organized and accomplish specific goals. Ask for: main goals, deadlines, existing obligations, preferred working hours and energy levels, and goal prioritization. Generate a day-by-day schedule with time blocks, explanation of how it leads to goal achievement, tool suggestions, motivation tips, check-in points, and small milestones.
