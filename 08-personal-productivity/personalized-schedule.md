# PERSONALIZED SCHEDULE BUILDER

**Builds a structured daily and weekly schedule optimized for your goals and energy patterns.**

---

Create a personalized schedule based on the inputs below.

**My profile:**

```
Primary goal: []  (e.g. studying for exams, building a project, job search)
Secondary goals: []
Wake time: []
Sleep time: []
Fixed commitments: []  (e.g. university 9–13:00 Mon/Wed/Fri, gym 18:00)
Energy pattern: [Morning person / Evening person / Consistent throughout day]
Current biggest time waste: []
Non-negotiables: []  (things that must stay regardless)
```

**Build the following:**

### 1. Weekly Template
A day-by-day schedule template showing:
- Fixed blocks (commitments, sleep, meals)
- Deep work blocks (primary goal)
- Shallow work blocks (admin, email, errands)
- Recovery and transition time

### 2. Daily Structure
For a typical day:
- Morning routine (wake → first task)
- Work/study blocks with specific time allocations
- Break protocol (frequency and length)
- Evening routine (last task → sleep)

### 3. Optimization Rules
- When to do deep cognitive work (based on energy pattern)
- When to do low-effort tasks
- How to handle interruptions
- What to do when motivation is low

### 4. Review System
- Daily review: what to check (5 minutes)
- Weekly review: what to assess (30 minutes)
- How to adjust the schedule when life disrupts it

### 5. First Week Plan
Map out day-by-day for the first 7 days to build momentum.
