# Employee Satisfaction Survey

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Analysis

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb814a9ff3f0ca3997b49e)

---

Create a comprehensive employee satisfaction survey for an organization. Include: overall job satisfaction questions, work environment and culture assessment, manager and leadership effectiveness questions, career development and growth opportunity ratings, compensation and benefits satisfaction, work-life balance assessment, communication and collaboration effectiveness, diversity and inclusion experience questions, open-ended feedback sections, scoring methodology and benchmark comparisons, and action planning recommendations based on results.
