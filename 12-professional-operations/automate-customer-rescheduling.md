# Automate Customer Rescheduling

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Marketing, General

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb81439ebad8637d262da7)

---

Develop a comprehensive plan to automate the workflow for handling customer requests to reschedule order deliveries using either Gemini or ChatGPT. Address: Data Extraction and Monitoring from emails/WhatsApp/SMS, Internal Relay of Information to production and logistics teams, Customer Confirmation and Communication, and Operational Considerations and Tools.
