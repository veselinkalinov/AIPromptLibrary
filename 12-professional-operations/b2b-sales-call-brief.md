# B2B Sales Call Brief

**Model:** Other | **Rating:** N/A | **Use Case:** Marketing

**Source:** [Notion](https://app.notion.com/320433d7bedb8145b022c31688d67db5)

---

Using Zoho CRM data fields, generate a concise, structured, and actionable B2B sales discovery call preparation brief. Based on Deal Name, Company Name, Industry, Product Category, Company Size, Website, Contact Name, Title, Notes, and Order History, generate: Company Overview, Likely Pain Points, Recommended Solutions/Cross-Sell Opportunities, Suggested Discovery Questions, and Recent News or Industry Insights.
